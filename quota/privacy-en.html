Privacy policy

Operator: Damian

Privacy and support contact: lsu011127@gmail.com

Effective date: October 2, 2026

QUOTA is an independent Android app operated by Damian. It helps you manage accounts you own, view available usage information, and run or schedule short warmup requests when supported. It is not an official app of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or GitHub.

Information stored on your device

The app stores linked account identifiers, any email or display name provided by the service, aliases, plan and quota observations, reset-credit details, token observations, schedules, settings, and execution records. OAuth tokens, refresh tokens, and API keys are encrypted using an Android Keystore key before being saved in app-private storage. QUOTA does not send these account records or credentials to a developer-operated cloud server.

Requests to AI services

Signing in, checking usage, and running warmup send the required authentication information to the service you choose. New ChatGPT plan registrations also use an opaque installation identifier and the app name. A warmup sends a short fixed model request and may consume subscription allowance or incur API charges. Enabled schedules can make these requests in the background. The respective service applies its own terms, billing rules, and privacy policy. Linked account details and AI credentials are not included in AdMob ad requests.

Advertising and privacy choices

QUOTA uses Google AdMob for the banner above the main tabs. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. When required, Google UMP presents privacy choices before ad requests. If a privacy-options entry point is required, you can revisit your choices in Settings. Advertising settings on your Android device can also reset or remove the advertising identifier. Third-party retention periods are governed by their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Linked credentials, account settings, and schedules remain on this device until you delete or replace them. Usage observations and completed execution records have a default retention of 90 days; Settings lets you choose 30, 90, or 180 days. Execution records with an uncertain result are kept until reviewed. Deleting a linked account removes its saved credentials and related local usage and execution records. Uninstalling the app removes its app-private data. Deleting a link does not delete your account with the AI service or revoke an authorization already held by that service; use its official settings to revoke access.

Your controls

You can delete accounts, remove schedules, pause automatic warmup, pause AI requests and hide the ad banner, change record retention, hide email addresses, and enable the device-authentication lock. App and device backups exclude QUOTA data. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions. Notification and biometric/device authentication permissions support the features you enable. These controls do not erase records already held by a service or advertising provider.

Support requests and changes

Contacting support opens your chosen email app; QUOTA does not automatically send a message or attach credentials. Information you voluntarily send is used to answer your request. Avoid sending API keys or OAuth tokens. If data handling changes, the app and published policy will be updated with a new effective date. For questions or deletion requests concerning information you sent to the operator, contact the address shown above.

Published policy website

The published policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the website. The policy page includes no advertising tags or analytics scripts. GitHub applies its own privacy statement to its hosting services.
