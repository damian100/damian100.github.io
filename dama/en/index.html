Find a notification again

Browse by app and conversation, search messages, collect photos and links, and bookmark records. All apps are included by default; you can configure exclusions or an allowed-app list.

An archive you manage on your device

Messages and images are stored in an encrypted database protected with Android Keystore. Optional device authentication, retention settings, record deletion and password-encrypted backup and restore help you manage the archive.

Only information provided in notifications

Archiving starts after notification access and archive consent. Dama cannot retrieve records from before installation or content hidden by the original app or Android. Images are saved only when provided and readable. Results depend on device and source-app settings.

Ads and privacy

Settings can display Google AdMob ads. Notification text and images are not added to ad requests. Ad SDK data practices are described in the privacy policy.

Support

If notifications are not archived, check notification access, archive consent and selected apps in Collection status. Force-stopping the app or revoking system access may require reconnecting.
